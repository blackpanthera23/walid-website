# Poliklinik Walid — References & Design North-Star

> Benchmark sites that match our situation: a **specialized service**, a **multi-location /
> corporate** identity, and a **lead-to-contact** flow (not e-commerce booking).

---

## The three references

### 1. Nuffield Health — `nuffieldhealth.com`  *(closest structural match)*
A UK healthcare **group** with many hospitals/clinics and specialties — structurally almost
identical to Poliklinik Walid.
- Site organised around **Treatments × Locations × Consultants** — the non-linear, self-select
  flow we want.
- Each location has its own rich page → the model for our 10 branch pages.
- Corporate-calm tone; trust signals live in the footer / "about" area, not everywhere.
- **Steal:** the Services × Branches × Doctors triangle as the whole-site backbone.

### 2. The London Clinic — `thelondonclinic.co.uk`  *(look & feel)*
A prestigious institution with many specialized departments — premium, corporate aesthetic.
- Premium-but-warm hero: real photography, whitespace, an **institution-first** headline
  (not a symptom).
- Services presented as corporate capability areas, drilling down to detail pages.
- Single calm, persistent primary CTA (enquire/contact).
- Credibility blocks (consultants, accreditations, stats) as reassurance.
- **Steal:** how to look premium and corporate while staying human.

### 3. Cleveland Clinic — `my.clevelandclinic.org`  *(information architecture)*
The gold standard for a large multi-specialty group's IA. Too big to copy, but the patterns
are the benchmark.
- **Find a Doctor / Find a Location / Explore Services** as the three dominant entry actions.
- Scannable service taxonomy — complex offering made simple via clear grouping.
- Trust via scale and outcomes, stated as facts up front.
- **Steal:** taxonomy + credibility-as-fact.

## The pattern all three converge on — our north-star

```
         ┌─────────────── THE TRIANGLE ───────────────┐
         │                                             │
    SERVICES  ◄───────────►  BRANCHES  ◄───────────►  DOCTORS
   (what we do)          (where / near me)         (who you'll see)
         │                     │                       │
         └──────────► single persistent CTA ◄──────────┘
                         (for us: WhatsApp us)
```

Principles this encodes:
1. **Non-linear** — the visitor self-selects their axis; no forced sequence.
2. **Corporate trust = scale + people + accreditation**, stated as facts up top.
3. **Compliance/caution → footer + one page**, never pervasive.
4. **One calm, persistent primary CTA.**
