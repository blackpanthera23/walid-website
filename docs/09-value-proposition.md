# Poliklinik Walid — Value Proposition & Positioning

> The "why Walid" layer that makes the structure mean something. This is what stops the site
> from being a generic corporate healthcare template. English-first. See `00-project-brief.md`.
>
> **Copy rules:** no em-dashes; avoid the "not a..., not a..." construction. Approved phrases:
> "hospital-grade care", "the smart choice", "care that respects your time and money",
> "day-care surgery".

---

## 1. Positioning statement

**Poliklinik Walid is surgeon-led day-care minor surgery that gives you hospital-grade care
at clinic speed and clinic prices, trusted by a community of hundreds of thousands.**

Positioning tier: **smart-value / premium-accessible.** Fair price, high quality, none of the
hospital hassle. Patients feel they made *the smart choice*.

### The one-liner (hero candidate)
> **"Hospital-grade minor surgery, done in a day. The smart choice hundreds of thousands already trust."**

Alternatives to A/B later:
- "Surgeon-led day-care surgery. Care that respects your time and money."
- "Minor surgery, done in a day. Hospital-grade care, the smart-value way."

## 2. The un-copyable distinction

Competitors can copy a layout, a palette, even a price. They cannot copy these four **earned
assets**, which is why the brand and the website must lead with them:

| Earned asset | Why it is un-copyable | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **Scaled following** (TikTok 561K · FB 119K · IG 30.8K) | Years of organic trust; competitors start from zero | Hero proof rail, proof wall, footer |
| **Review depth** (4.9 from 1,098 Google) | Volume + rating together signal *consistency*, not luck | Reviews block (stat + quotes) |
| **Surgeon-led surgical centres** (Central: Ampang · North: Jalan Baru, Tasek Gelugor, Sungai Petani) | Real two-hub concentration of expertise (see `11-pillars-and-specialists.md`) | Hero, Minor Surgery, Branches |
| **Day-care efficiency at clinic price** | A genuine operating model, not a slogan | Value section, Minor Surgery |

**Rule for every touchpoint:** lead with what cannot be copied. Structure (triangle, pages) is
the skeleton; these four are the muscle.

---

## 3. The value / efficiency story (approved language)

Frame value as respect for the patient's **time and money**, delivered at hospital-grade quality.

- **Day-care surgery** — assessed and treated, home the same day.
- **No referral needed** — book straight through, self-refer.
- **Hospital-grade care** — surgeon-led, MMC-registered doctors, sterile day-care setup.
- **Care that respects your time and money** — clinic speed, clinic prices, honest value.
- **The smart choice** — the value framing we use instead of "cheap" or "worth it".

Indicative procedure pricing RM2,000 to RM3,500 (confirm per-procedure wording before publishing).

> Positioning tier reminder: **smart-value / premium-accessible.** Never "budget". The message is
> *high quality made accessible*, not *cheap*.

---

## 4. Medical tourism (staged pillar, launch later)

Architect now, launch when ready (same approach as i18n in brief §8).

- **Now (launch):** an English "International Patients" page that signals Walid already serves
  patients from Sudan, Singapore, Indonesia and Brunei. Perception first.
- **Later:** full medical-tourism offer (travel/logistics help, package wording, language
  support, international testimonials).
- **Why it fits the value prop:** smart-value + hospital-grade + huge social proof is exactly
  what a cross-border patient weighs. Medical tourism is the value prop extended across a border.
