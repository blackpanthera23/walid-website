## 0. The full cross-touchpoint journey (this is the real experience)

The website is one surface in a longer journey. Most patients meet Walid on **TikTok first**,
not the homepage. Maximum experience means **one consistent brand across every surface**, with
no drop in quality from the viral video to the clinic chair.

```
DISCOVER          VERIFY            EVALUATE          ACT               DELIVER
TikTok / FB / IG  Google reviews    Website           WhatsApp          Clinic visit
561K / 119K /     4.9 · 1,098       (this project)    (general or       (day-care
30.8K             reviews                             minor-surgery)     surgery)
  │                 │                 │                 │                 │
  └─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
        SAME THREE SIGNALS CARRIED AT EVERY STAGE, NO DROP IN QUALITY:
        1. Surgeon-led   2. Day-care, worth it (hospital-grade, smart choice)   3. Loved at scale
```

**Consistency rule:** whatever a visitor felt on TikTok must be reinforced on the site, in the
WhatsApp reply, and at the clinic. If the website feels more generic than the TikTok that
brought them in, trust drops and the journey breaks. Perception is managed by *consistency*.

The sections below detail the **website** portion (Arrive to Contact) inside this larger arc.

## The emotional arc

```
Arrive            Orient            Evaluate          Decide            Contact
  │                 │                 │                 │                 │
"Are they         "What & where      "Can I trust     "How do I        "Handled."
 legit?"           are they?"         them?"           reach them?"
  │                 │                 │                 │                 │
SCALE &           SERVICES &        PEOPLE &         WHATSAPP          CALM
CREDIBILITY       NETWORK           PROOF            (frictionless)    FOLLOW-UP
```

The arc only goes one direction: **upward confidence.** Each stage adds credibility; the
conversion is a single familiar tap. Caution and clinical caveats are kept out of the path.

## Stage-by-stage

| Stage | Visitor mindset | What they need | Our move | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Arrive** | "Is this legitimate & capable?" | Instant signal of scale & professionalism | Corporate hero: the group, its network, its reach | WhatsApp (ambient) |
| **2. Orient** | "What do they do, near me?" | Clear services + branch network | Services overview + branch network | Explore / WhatsApp |
| **3. Evaluate** | "Can I trust them?" | Credibility, scale, people | Doctors, branch count, panels, reviews stat | WhatsApp |
| **4. Decide** | "How do I reach them?" | A frictionless, familiar channel | Persistent WhatsApp, routed by enquiry type | **WhatsApp us** |
| **5. Contact** | "Message sent." | Confidence it'll be handled | Clear channels; team follows up | — |

### 1 — Arrive (corporate first impression)
- **Hero lean = network / reach**, with the center-of-excellence nuance (see brief §7):
  *"A healthcare group with reach — with a specialist hub for minor surgery."*
  Sell the group's access/reassurance, while positioning minor surgery as concentrated at
  the main clinic(s), not uniform across all 10 branches.
- Scale stated as **facts, not warnings:** 10 branches · trained doctors · 20+ registered panels.
- Premium corporate visuals, real photography, no red emergency bar.

### 2 — Orient (what + where)
- **Services** as high-level capability areas (General Medicine · Minor Surgery · Women's &
  Family Health). Detailed selling pages come later.
- **Branch network** as evidence of reach + the practical "find one near me" layer.

### 3 — Evaluate (credibility)
- **Doctors / people** — a corporate "our team" feel builds more trust than disclaimers.
- **Scale & coverage** — branches, panels, years operating (if available).
- **Reviews** — 4.9 / 5 · 1,098 Google reviews, presented as a corporate credibility stat.

### 4 — Decide (convert)
- Persistent, on-brand WhatsApp CTA on every page (confident, not anxious).
- Routing by enquiry type (see `03-patient-flow.md` §WhatsApp routing).

### 5 — Contact (handover)
- One calm line that the clinic team responds and helps choose a branch/slot. No wall of caveats.

## Where trust / compliance content lives (contained)

- **Footer:** short boilerplate only — MMC-registered doctors · PDPA 2010 confidentiality ·
  (one quiet emergency line only if legally required).
- **One dedicated page** (*About / Governance* or *Patient Information*): the fuller compliance,
  privacy, and medical-disclaimer content.
- **Nowhere else** — no banners, no per-section warnings.
