## Primary navigation (header)

`Home · About · Services · Branches · Doctors · Contact`  +  persistent **WhatsApp us** button.

Keep it to these 6 + CTA. Corporate sites stay lean — resist adding more.

## Page intents

| Page | Job | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Establish the group's scale & credibility; route to the triangle | WhatsApp |
| **About** | Corporate story + network rationale; house compliance on a sub-page | WhatsApp |
| **Services** | Show 3 capability pillars at a high level | Explore / WhatsApp |
| **Branches** | Prove reach; help find the nearest; pivot to contact | WhatsApp (per branch) |
| **Doctors** | Build trust through people | WhatsApp |
| **Contact** | Present WhatsApp channels clearly; help choose a branch | WhatsApp |

## Notes
- **Branch pages are the SEO + conversion engine** — one per location, with address, hours,
  services offered there, map, photos, and a branch-routed WhatsApp CTA.
- **Services pages stay high-level for now.** Per-service selling pages (lipoma, cyst,
  pregnancy scan, etc.) come in a later phase.
- **Compliance has exactly one home** (About → Governance & patient information) + footer.
