# Poliklinik Walid — Homepage Wireframe & Draft Copy

> Section-by-section corporate homepage blueprint with draft copy. Built on the locked
> strategy (brief §7, value prop, pillars, proof). English-first. WhatsApp-only.
> **Copy rules:** no em-dashes; avoid "not a..., not a...". Draft copy is for the consultant
> to finalise (owner writes content).

---

## Design intent (one line)
A calm, premium corporate page that leads with scale and proof, presents minor surgery as the
hero with aesthetics and antenatal as rising pillars, and converts to one WhatsApp tap. Mobile-first.

## Section-by-section draft copy

### 0. Header
- Logo (left). Nav: Services · Branches · Doctors · About · Contact.
- Persistent **WhatsApp us** button (brand-styled, confident).

### 1. Hero
- **Headline (draft):** "Hospital-grade minor surgery, done in a day."
- **Subline (draft):** "Surgeon-led day-care care across our Central and Northern centres. The smart choice hundreds of thousands already trust."
- **CTAs:** `[WhatsApp us]` (primary) · `[Explore services]` (secondary)
- **Proof rail:** `561K on TikTok · 119K on Facebook · 4.9 from 1,098 Google reviews`
- Visual: restrained brand composition at launch; swap to real consented photography when supplied.

### 2. Value strip
Three short points (icons + a few words each), using approved language:
1. **Day-care surgery** — "Assessed and treated, home the same day."
2. **Hospital-grade care** — "Surgeon-led, MMC-registered doctors."
3. **The smart choice** — "Care that respects your time and money."

### 3. Hero pillar: Minor Surgery
- **Headline (draft):** "Minor surgery, done the smart-value way."
- **Body (draft):** "Lipoma, cysts, moles, skin tags, warts and more, assessed and treated in a day. Performed at our Central centre in Ampang and our Northern centres in Seberang Perai, Tasek Gelugor and Sungai Petani."
- **CTA:** `[WhatsApp the minor surgery team]`

### 4. Rising pillars ("also expert in...")
Section label (draft): "Also expert in." Three cards, smaller weight:
- **Aesthetic procedures** — short line `[confirm scope]`
- **Antenatal and women's health** — "Pregnancy scans from 7 weeks, led by a dedicated specialist."
- **[Pillar 4 — TBD]** — placeholder card, ready to fill.

### 5. Specialist spotlight (expertise-first)
- **Headline (draft):** "Antenatal care, led by a dedicated specialist."
- **Proof line:** "Led by Dr Anis, [O&G credential]." `[confirm credential]`

### 6. Proof / social wall
- 2 to 3 embedded TikToks of real, consented procedures/stories (lazy-loaded).
- Short caption tying it to the value prop. Link out to the TikTok profile.

### 7. Branch network (two-hub)
- **Headline (draft):** "A network you can reach, with surgical centres Central and North."
- Central: Ampang. North: Jalan Baru (Seberang Perai), Tasek Gelugor, Sungai Petani.
- Line: "General practice across the full network." `[CTA: Find a branch]`

### 8. Reviews
- **Stat headline:** "4.9 from 1,098 Google reviews."
- 2 to 3 curated, permission-cleared quotes (see `08-owner-decisions.md`).

### 9. International patients (soft branding)
- **Line (draft):** "Patients travel from Sudan, Singapore, Indonesia and Brunei for care with us."
- Small link to the International Patients page. Keep it light at launch (medical tourism is staged).

### 10. Final CTA
- **Line (draft):** "Ready when you are. Message us on WhatsApp and the clinic team will help."
- `[WhatsApp us]`

### 11. Footer
- Nav repeat · working social icons (TikTok, FB, IG, YouTube, Google) · "MMC-registered doctors"
  · "Patient records kept confidential under the PDPA 2010" · "Walid Healthcare Group Sdn. Bhd.
  (1466692-A)" · emergency line only if legally required (quiet, not a banner).

## Mobile notes
- Hero: headline, subline, one primary WhatsApp CTA, then the proof rail stacked.
- Pillars and branches stack to single column; keep the minor-surgery block visually dominant.
- Floating WhatsApp button always visible.

---

## Build notes (WordPress / Kadence)
- Each section becomes a **Gutenberg pattern** so staff can reorder/edit.
- Pillars, specialist spotlight, branches, reviews pull from CPTs (see `05-content-model.md`),
  so content updates do not require touching layout.
- Social embeds lazy-loaded; never render-blocking.
